# Privacy Policy for TandaXn

**Last updated:** September 4, 2026

TandaXn ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the TandaXn mobile application (the "App"). Please read this policy carefully.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide and improve our savings‑circle management service.

- **Account Data:** When you create an account, we ask for your **email address, full name, and phone number**. You may also upload a **profile photo**.
- **KYC Data:** For certain features (withdrawals, advances), we may collect identity verification documents as required by law. These are retained temporarily for verification.
- **Usage Data:** We collect anonymised data about how you interact with the App (feature usage, screen views).
- **Device Data:** Device model, OS version, and unique identifiers for analytics and crash reporting – in aggregated, non‑identifiable form.

We do **not** collect precise geolocation, contacts, or photos (except your chosen profile photo).

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data for:
- Creating and managing your account.
- Providing core functionality (circles, contributions, payouts, XnScore).
- Sending service notifications (contribution reminders, payout confirmations).
- Maintaining circle integrity.
- Complying with legal obligations (e.g., anti‑money laundering).
- Improving the App through analytics.

We will **never** sell, rent, or trade your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

---

## 2.5 Automated Decision‑Making (XnScore, Circle Reputation, Honor Score)

TandaXn uses automated scoring algorithms to calculate your **XnScore, Circle Reputation, and Honor Score**. These scores help determine eligibility for certain features and circle participation.

- **What data feeds into these scores:** Payment history, circle completion rates, savings behavior, community vouches, and account tenure.
- **Your rights:** If you receive an **adverse decision** (e.g., account suspension, circle removal, or feature restriction) based on these scores, you have the right to:
  - Request a **human review** of the decision.
  - Receive an **explanation** of the specific factors that led to the decision.
  - **Challenge and correct** the data used in the scoring.
- To invoke these rights, contact us at **privacy@tandaxn.com** (see Section 6).

## 3.5 Regulatory Compliance & Financial Reporting

TandaXn is **not** a bank and does not hold a Money Transmitter License (MTL). All fund movements are processed by our third‑party partner, **Stripe**.

However, we are subject to the **Bank Secrecy Act (BSA)** and **Anti‑Money Laundering (AML)** regulations. Where legally required:

- We may file **Suspicious Activity Reports (SARs)** or **Currency Transaction Reports (CTRs)** with FinCEN or state financial regulators.
- Law enforcement and regulators may request data, and we must comply.
- **Important:** Due to these regulations, we are prohibited from notifying users when a SAR or CTR is filed.

## 4. Security of Your Information

We implement industry‑standard security measures:

- **Encryption in transit (TLS/SSL)** for all data transfer.
- **Encryption at rest** for sensitive data in our database.
- **Access controls** – only authorized personnel can access user data.
- Regular security audits.

No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure; you are responsible for keeping your login credentials confidential.

---

## 4.4 Data Breach Notification

If we become aware of a breach involving unauthorized access, disclosure, or acquisition of your personal information:

- We will notify affected users **without undue delay** and within required regulatory timeframes (e.g., 72 hours to EU regulators under GDPR, and state‑specific timelines for US users).
- Notifications will describe:
  - The nature of the breach.
  - The categories of data affected.
  - The actions we have taken.
  - Steps you can take to protect yourself.
  - Contact information for further questions.

## 5. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g., GDPR for EU residents), you have the right to:

- **Access:** Request a copy of your personal data.
- **Correction:** Update or correct inaccurate information.
- **Deletion:** Delete your account (see Section 6.5 below).
- **Data Portability:** Export your data in a machine‑readable format.
- **Withdraw Consent:** Where we rely on consent, withdraw it at any time.

To exercise these rights, contact **privacy@tandaxn.com**. We respond within 30 days.

---

## 6.5 Data Retention & Account Deletion (Granularity)

### Account Deletion Process
- You can delete your account from the App (**Settings → Delete Account**).
- A **30‑day grace period** begins. You may cancel the deletion by contacting support during this window.
- After 30 days, the deletion becomes final.

### What Happens After Final Deletion
- **Personal Identifiable Information (PII)** – name, email, phone, profile photo – is **permanently removed**.
- **Anonymised historical data** (transaction history, circle records, XnScore calculations) is **retained** to preserve circle integrity and score continuity. This data is no longer linked to you personally.

### Data Retention Periods (Specific)
| Data Category | Retention Period |
| :--- | :--- |
| Financial transaction records | **7 years** (regulatory/IRS requirement) |
| KYC verification data | **5 years** post‑account‑closure (BSA/AML) |
| XnScore historical data | **24 months** post‑account‑closure |
| Communication logs (support tickets) | **3 years** |
| Marketing preferences | Until user opts out or account is deleted |

**Note:** If you request a copy of your retained data before deletion, we will provide it within 30 days.

## 7. AI / Machine Learning Model Training

**We do NOT use your personal data to train generative AI models** (e.g., ChatGPT, large language models).

Our proprietary XnScore algorithms are based on **deterministic, pre‑defined statistical rules**. Refinements to these algorithms are performed exclusively on **de‑identified, aggregated data** – never on individual user records. No individual user data is used to train or refine models.
